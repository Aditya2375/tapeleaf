WHAT THIS TERMINAL KNOWS

Trade, quote, aggregate.
Keep them separate.

Invented inputs

ALFA, BRIO, COVE, DUNE, ELIO and FENN are fictional labels, not real listings. There is no exchange, currency, timezone or clock. Tick 1 is the first simulated event, not a minute. Every event creates one trade and one quote for each symbol.

Integer price steps, sizes and symmetric quotes come from xorshift32. Mixed steps lie from −20 to +20 cents. Alternating drifts add +9 cents for ALFA/COVE/ELIO, −9 for the others. All prices are floored at 1 fictitious unit. Flat prices keep all trade prices at their prior closes, while size and spread still change.

Exact tape accounting

Bid = trade − h; ask = trade + h, with h from 1 to 8 cents. Spread = ask − bid. Spread bps = 10,000 × spread / quote midpoint. Constructing quotes this way is artificial; it does not show fill probability, depth or liquidity.

Visible VWAP = Σ(trade price × size) / Σsize, from tick 1 through the cursor. It uses every invented trade, not an OHLC typical-price approximation. OHLCV groups consecutive events into 5, 10 or 20 tick bars. The current bar is partial until its last event is visible.

Boundaries

No live or delayed prices, real market data, predictions, orders, broker keys, accounts, uploads, saved settings, analytics or external runtime requests. No profits, returns strategy, recommendations or evidence of market realism.

Only the visible prefix enters displayed metrics, sorting, breadth, bars and recent trades. The whole deterministic tape exists in memory; this is a learning replay, not a secure future-data barrier or a backtest. Refresh resets the session.
